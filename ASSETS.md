### Files the new "screencap" background category loads

The picker (`ConfigSidebar.tsx`, `BackgroundSettingsPopover.tsx`) lists a single tab "Screencap" and wallpaper ids `screencap/1` to `screencap/6`. The app resolves `assets/backgrounds/<id>.jpg` as the full image and the same name with `-thumbnail.jpg` as the thumbnail. The default background for new recordings is `screencap/1.jpg`. Required files in `apps/desktop/src-tauri/assets/backgrounds/screencap/`:

- `1.jpg` ... `6.jpg`: JPEG, 2560x1440 (16:9), progressive, about 300 to 500 KB each.
- `1-thumbnail.jpg` ... `6-thumbnail.jpg`: JPEG, 256x144, baseline, about 5 to 12 KB each.
